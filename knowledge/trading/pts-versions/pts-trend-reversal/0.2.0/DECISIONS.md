## Geometry, identity and information time

Pivot prices are anchored at their candle open times; their separate confirmation times determine
when geometry becomes usable. The weekly base is an exact rational interpolation between the
last two confirmed directional extrema, with the opposite parallel through the farthest confirmed
extreme between them. Daily closes are tested at their close times for invalidation. ATR tolerances
retain rational comparisons; stored stop margins round away from C.

A setup key includes verified market ID, direction and A/B/C original times and prices.
Only a confirmed opening consumes it, atomically with the fill. A close never frees it.
Rank by confirmation time then numerical market ID, veto after selection and never replace
blocked candidates on final refresh. Final refresh can only cancel or preserve a chosen opening;
it cannot promote a hold, reverse direction or substitute anchors. Each portfolio remains independent.

Native history queries preserve response bodies, original times, blocks, reception dates, holes
and hashes. Later retrieved history is a versioned projection of recorded candles, not proof
of the exact historical request bytes or revision availability. Weekly/monthly aggregation never
fills absent days. Missing minimum history disables that market.

## Versions and scope

The course, candidate descriptor, policy and implementation commit have distinct identities.
PTS 0.2.0 is reachable only through local evaluation v8, not Studio or deployed agents.
SMA remains original hourly 1.0.0 with selective risk, 2% individual stop and four-hour horizon.
The future comparison evaluates complete strategies, including their different exits;
it does not isolate only entry quality.

No public comparison starts during this implementation. Technical tests do not establish
market coverage or profitability. No candle-only counterfactual P&L is calculated.
