# 01 — Public marketplace, paid reads and their pricing

Research against `codex/product-scope-map` = `origin/main` at `c407fec`, 2026-09-30. Read-only.
Every file:line below was read on that tree.

## 1. Summary

1. Every marketplace route exists and renders, and all except `/markets` and `/` now read Postgres through `/v1/marketplace/*`. With an empty database they show honest empties: settlement, reads and following are unbuilt, so the adapter hard-codes `0`/`null`/`[]` (`packages/infrastructure/src/persistence/agent-directory.ts:122,191,233,248-253,277-284`).
2. A paid read is fully modelled in core (`packages/core/src/features/agents/domain/reads.ts`) and in the DTOs (`packages/api-schema/src/agents.ts:95-150`): one fixed 24h question, two clocks (delivery SLA with refund, then market evaluation), a price in AUSD. Nothing persists it, and no route, schema or payment rail writes one.
3. Nobody can set the price of a consultation. No studio screen, API field, table or spec covers who sets the price, SLA, markets or slots, or how. Go Public is the natural home, and it is itself unspecified ("Soon").
4. The UI is built and then stops honestly: the ask flow and the market ask-ticket end at a disabled Pay button that says "Nothing is charged today" (`ask-flow.tsx:185-205`, `ask-ticket.tsx:104-115`). Because `service` is always `null`, `/agents/[h]/ask` always redirects to the profile (`app/agents/[handle]/ask/page.tsx:37-39`).
5. Wallet SIWE sign-in and linking are real (`workers/api/src/routes/wallet-auth.ts:77-126`). The portfolio shows only the native balance for real. `/reads` and the portfolio's reads tab fetch the global read list, not the wallet's own. The three-host split (`product-hosts-v1`) is not implemented: `hosts.ts` still has two hosts with the old names.

## 3. Gaps

### Specified but not built

- **Paid-read persistence and lifecycle.** Specified piecemeal: the domain in `reads.ts`; the owner rule "a paid read belongs to a `user_id` and the email that hears about its outcome is a field on the profile" (user-profiles-and-products-v1 §8, lines 286-293); the endpoints `POST /v1/reads` (paid) and `GET /v1/reads[/:id]` plus "a settlement job at delivery + 24 h" (public-data-audit table, line 255). None of it is built. No spec owns it end to end.
- **The email captured before buying a read.** The profile field is named (user-profiles §2 line 122, the §8 table) and does not exist.
- **Three-host split** (product-hosts-v1 §6): `hosts.ts`, the landing product, the AASA-per-host rule, `TURNKEY_RPID=studio.pickler.fun`, the marketplace account sheet's wallets section.
- **`/v1/demo` and its alias removal** (marketplace-real-data §5).
- **Empty states with ids** (marketplace-real-data §4 wants five). Some exist (reads "Nothing in this state." at `reads-page.tsx:100`). Checking each route against the design harness is left to the design agent.
- **Sample labelling on `/markets`** (public-data-audit gap 5; `docs/frontend-integration.md:39-40` "existing sample data must stay visibly labelled"). The perps board shows invented agents-reading counts with no tag.

### Needed but not specified

- **Read pricing and the offer ("read service").** Who sets `price`, `sla`, `typical`, `slots` and `markets`, and where (studio Go Public? a separate "Sell reads" section?). Bounds (min/max AUSD), whether the platform takes a fee, whether a closed or capacity-limited state is manual or computed (`slots` is "as the agent reports it", `reads.ts:36-37`). `ReadService` has the fields, and no writer or spec exists.
- **Payment rail.** Its options are unexamined: AUSD `transferWithAuthorization` (EIP-3009, which the chain package already verifies), an escrow contract, or x402 (explicitly excluded from the Relayer port). Also open: where the money goes, what releases it at delivery, how the refund is executed on SLA miss, who pays gas, and testnet vs mainnet. The audit lists "Payment destination for a read" as Missing (line 238). The UI says only "Paid reads open with the testnet contracts" (`ask-flow.tsx:204`), which contracts is unspecified.
- **The delivery pipeline.** What turns a paid question into an answer: an on-demand research run for that agent (admission, budget, the queue in `workers/research`), the mapping from run output to `ReadAnswer{probability, summary, reasons, sources, limits, reference}`, and timeouts against the SLA. The audit frames it as "a publication of a research run … a publish step and a payment" (lines 113-115), but on-demand runs per buyer are not the same as publishing a scheduled run.
- **The evaluation job (second clock).** A reference price at delivery, an observation at +24h within a tolerance window (`not_evaluable`), and which price source applies (Perpl mark, or Pons for prediction markets, `reads.ts:106-118`).
- **Settlement / public record** (marketplace-real-data §7 names it as its own spec): picks projected from `pickler.runs.decision`, resolved outcomes, hit rate, calibration, and the leaderboard score (`agentScore`, `reads.ts:158-167`). This blocks the leaderboard, analytics, pick detail, the activity log and the read service's `evaluated`/`gap`.
- **The publish bridge** (public-data-audit order item 5): which private research events become public activity and picks, when, and with what redaction. It gates `/activity`, persona `decisions`, receipts and the market squares' `calls`.
- **Per-wallet reads.** `listReads()` takes no viewer. `/reads` and the portfolio need the authenticated user (SIWE session → `user_id`). Separately, public read cards (`isPublic`) need a public listing: two different queries that the port conflates.
- **Following** as a server feature (marketplace-real-data §2.3 defers it; today it is localStorage only).
- **Perps board from a real venue** (a Perpl client behind `MarketBoard`; the infrastructure already has `perpl/data.ts`). The audit called this the shortest path. No spec exists; marketplace-real-data §7 says "its own work".
- **Horizons other than 24h** (the "4h · SOON" and "7d · SOON" tags at `ask-flow.tsx:109-110`) contradict the comparability rule in `readQuestion`. That needs a product decision before anything is built.

## 4. Specs to write

1. **`paid-reads-v1.md` — the read, end to end.** Scope: the `reads` schema (question, venue/market/level, agent, buyer `user_id`, payer address, price, `isPublic`, context text, delivery state and deadline, answer JSON with sources, reference price, evaluation state and outcome) plus idempotency. `POST /v1/reads` (authenticated by the SIWE session, requires the profile email), `GET /v1/reads` (mine), `GET /v1/marketplace/reads` (public cards), `GET /v1/reads/:id` (visibility rules). The delivery orchestration (enqueue an on-demand research run, map it to `ReadAnswer`, SLA timer, failure → refund), the notification email, and the UI states the portfolio already draws (`IN WINDOW`, `SETTLED · HAPPENED`, `EXPIRED · REFUNDED`). Depends on: the payment spec (2), the read-service spec (3), the research queue/admission in `workers/research`, the profile email field, and the evaluation spec (4) for the second clock.
2. **`read-payments-v1.md` — the payment rail.** Scope: choose the mechanism (EIP-3009 authorization to an escrow or to Pickler, or an escrow contract with release on delivery and refund on SLA miss, vs x402), the chain (Monad testnet 10143 first?), the recipient and revenue split (agent owner / platform fee / buyback, cf. the "10% of every settled win" copy cited in public-data-audit line 188), refund execution and who pays gas. Also the ledger reconciliation (reuse `budget.ledger_entries`/audit, or a new table), and the fact that the marketplace wallet never signs today (apps/web/AGENTS.md:84), so this spec also decides whether the marketplace starts asking wallets to sign. Depends on: the AUSD metadata in `@pickler/chain` (done) and the contracts owner. It is independent of settlement.
3. **`read-service-v1.md` — configuring the price of a consultation.** Scope: where the agent owner turns reads on (studio Go Public step or its own section), the fields (`price` in AUSD with bounds, the list of markets from the agent's `marketScope`, the SLA window, capacity/slots, open/closed with a note), storage (a new `agents.read_services` table or `agent_profiles` columns, written only by the studio API and read by `PostgresAgentDirectory`), validation in core, the DTO in `api-schema` (mirrored bounds, following the `PERP_LIMITS_DTO` precedent), what "typical" is computed from, and whether an agent must be funded, public or non-draft to sell. Depends on: the Go Public spec (voice/X/wallet, currently "Soon" in studio-brief-v1 §9). It is a prerequisite for (1).
4. **`settlement-and-record-v1.md` — picks, evaluation and the public record.** Scope: the publish bridge from `pickler.runs.decision` to public picks, resolution for Polymarket/Pons and Perpl, the read-evaluation job (+24h, tolerance, `not_evaluable`), the aggregates (hit rate, calibration gap, net, `agentScore`), the leaderboard and analytics, the pick detail and receipt, and the `/activity` log. Named but unwritten in marketplace-real-data §7. Depends on: venue price sources and paper/live order records (PR #46 paper settlement exists for sports).
5. **`marketplace-activity-and-following-v1.md`.** Scope: which agent events are public (runs, passes, trades, reads answered), their redaction, server-side follows keyed to `user_id` (replacing localStorage), and a follower count. Depends on: (4) for picks and the SIWE session.
6. **`perps-board-live-v1.md`.** Scope: a `MarketBoard` adapter over the existing Perpl public-data client (`packages/infrastructure/src/perpl/`), caching, a sample/stale label until then, and a source for `agentsReading`/`openCalls` (needs (4)). Depends on: PR #63/#60 (Perpl data reliability), both open.
7. **Implementation of product-hosts-v1** is already specified. It needs a plan, not a spec, but it gates the `SIWE_DOMAIN` value and the phone app.

## 5. Open questions for the owner

1. Who sets the read price: the agent owner freely, within platform bounds, or the platform? Is there a platform fee on reads, and does any of it go to the agent's token (buyback)?
2. Payment rail: AUSD via EIP-3009 authorization, an escrow contract, or x402? On which chain first? Does "the testnet contracts" in the Pay note refer to the launchpad contracts or to new read contracts?
3. Refunds: automatic on-chain on an SLA miss, or credited? Who pays gas for a refund?
4. Is a read answered by a fresh on-demand research run (costs model/provider budget per buyer: who pays that, the read price?) or by publishing the agent's latest scheduled run?
5. Must a buyer give an email before paying (user-profiles §8 says yes)? Is a SIWE session mandatory to buy, or can an anonymous wallet pay?
6. Public vs private read cards: does a private read still count in the agent's public record (the DTO comment says "only counts in the aggregate")?
7. Keep the fixed 24h question and drop the "4h/7d SOON" tags, or allow multiple horizons (which breaks comparability)?
8. Can a draft or unfunded agent sell reads, or only after Go Public?
9. `/reads` is described both as "the questions you paid for" (wallet-gated) and as a public set of cards. Should there be one page or two?
10. Ship `/markets` still unlabelled sample content, or label it or hide it until Perpl data is wired?
11. When is product-hosts-v1 implemented? `SIWE_DOMAIN` and the mobile app's values depend on it.
