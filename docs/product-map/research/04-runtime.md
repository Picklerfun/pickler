# 04 — Agent runtime, decisions and their review

Scope: the research runtime (Mastra), decisions and the policy over them, the run trace, the
laboratories that review decisions (Jev, BTC benchmark, comparisons), scheduling, budgets,
providers, the agent HMAC API and what is deployed. Read-only map of `main` at `c407fec`.

## 1. Summary

1. The runtime is real and shared: one core runner (`packages/core/src/features/research/run-research.ts`) runs under local Node/Mastra Studio and the deployed Cloudflare Queue/Cron consumer (`workers/research`), with leases, quotas, versioned prompts, a policy layer and a persisted event trace. The runtime only runs **sports markets on Polymarket**.
2. A decision is `TRADE`/`ABSTAIN` from the model, then rechecked by deterministic core policy (uncertainty, margin, fresh ask, and since studio-brief-v1 the brief's entry/never rules). The owner can inspect every run's trace and decision in the Studio. **The public marketplace shows none of it:** picks, decisions, reads and the record are empty on purpose.
3. "Reviewing decisions" exists only as **local operator laboratories**: Jev/DeepSeek classifier evals v1–v4, runtime decision observation, Jev-first paper studies, the BTC v3 paired comparison (the only one completed: 32/32 markets, virtual P&L −4.84) and the v4 benchmark (stopped on invalid Jev answers). None of these is a product feature, a hosted runtime path or a UI.
4. Deployment: the research Worker is deployed to staging (Hyperdrive, Queue, 1-minute Cron). The recorded remote acceptance **failed** (2 runs, `PROVIDER_OR_MODEL_FAILURE`, fixed later by the transport correction), and no successful remote run is recorded since. Mastra Studio, all laboratories, Jev and decision observation are local only.
5. Largest gaps: no product surface for reviewing or grading decisions, no decision-resolution record (so no hit rate or calibration), no publishing of decisions to the marketplace, no agent-issued HMAC credentials or write endpoints, no monetary budget on model/search spend, no follow-ups or calendar schedules, and no market scope beyond sports.

## 3. Gaps

**Reviewing decisions (revisar las decisiones)**

- No product feature reviews decisions. The Studio shows one decision at a time (`sections/picks.tsx`), but the owner cannot mark it right, wrong or bad reasoning, and nothing persists a review.
- Nothing resolves a decision: no job reads the market outcome and scores `modelAssessment.probability` against it, abstentions included. Paper settlement only covers filled paper orders and only when triggered by hand (`studio.ts:663`). So hit rate, Brier, calibration, `resolved`, `net` and the leaderboard have no source (`agent-directory.ts:191-196`).
- Jev exists as three local adapters (claim classifier, readiness router, quantitative veto) and is not in the hosted path. Its reliability as a reviewer is unproven. On synthetic material contradictions the current reviewer caught 0/6 and the compact one 5/6 (development only), and strict-invariant failures stopped both v4 runs. The earlier classifier evals (16/16 holdout) are 32 author-written synthetic cases.
- The experiments measure different things: evidence-sufficiency classification (v1–v4), semantic contradiction veto (benchmark), policy economics (v3), strategy P&L (perps). None of them feeds a product metric.

**Showing agents' actions (mostrar las acciones de los agentes)**

- The marketplace has no public projection of runs. Nothing decides which trace fields may be public: the spec separates "public evidence" from "internal traces and private sources" (agent-runtime-v1 l.273), but that is not built. The profile's `decides`/`wrong` fields are author text, not runtime output.
- Events are fetched whole with no cursor. There is no push (spec l.253 allows polling first) and no run cancel endpoint.
- The trace text is web-only (`trace.ts`). A public page would need the same mapping, reviewed for leaking private config (for example the brief, which is private configuration, marketplace-real-data-v1 §2.2).

**Runtime**

- Market scope is sports-only. Perpl/BTC exist only in laboratories and are `SOON_VENUES` in the Studio (studio-brief-v1 §3.5).
- Exit rules are recorded, never executed.
- No monetary budget covers model and search spend. The API has a micro-USD ledger that the runner does not use.
- Follow-ups, calendar schedules and event-driven research are missing.
- Remote acceptance: the only recorded paid staging runs failed (`docs/staging-deployment-2026-09-20.md:163-190`). The transport fix is deployed (`:249-263`), but no successful remote manual or scheduled run is recorded. `ADMISSIONS_ENABLED` is `"true"` in `workers/research/wrangler.jsonc` for operator-approved users (`docs/cloudflare-staging.md:136`).
- Agent HMAC API: verifier only. No secret issuance, rotation, kill, events ingestion or write routes. No Pickler agent (the runtime runs in-process) calls this API today, so its product purpose is undefined.

## 4. Specs to write

1. **Decision resolution and record v1.** For every completed decision (TRADE and ABSTAIN), read the official resolution (Polymarket/Polygon, as `PolymarketPaperResolution` already does) and write an immutable resolution row. Score it: correct side, Brier on the estimate, whether it was inside the range, and virtual P&L when a paper order filled. Define aggregates (hit rate, calibration buckets, `resolved`, `net`) and their minimum sample. This unblocks marketplace-real-data-v1 §7 (leaderboard, analytics, pick detail). Depends on the decision shapes v2–v4, paper settlement (`packages/trading/src/features/paper/settlement.ts`) and a Cron/Queue job in `workers/research` or `workers/trading`.
2. **Public decision projection ("picks") v1.** Choose which run and decision fields go public, and when: only after market close, or after a delay, to protect the edge of paid reads. Cover how sources and quotes are shown, what is redacted (brief, skills, private sources, token usage, diagnostics), whether abstentions are shown, and how the trace becomes a public "what it did" timeline. Also cover the marketplace endpoints `profiles/:ticker/picks/:id` and `decisions`. Depends on spec 1, marketplace-real-data-v1, paid reads (`pickler-paid-reads` memory), and the Go Public sub-project (a draft agent stays private).
3. **Decision review in the Studio v1 (product).** Let the owner, and later a reviewer, review an agent's decisions: a list with filters (action, policy reason, rule that held it, resolved or not), per-decision grading (right/wrong/unclear, reasoning quality, source problems) stored as human labels, and a view of patterns over time. Optionally include an automated second opinion (Jev) marked "assistant review", never "human". Define storage, who may review, and how labels feed the evaluation datasets (`prepare:decision-evidence` expects human labels). Depends on spec 1, the Studio `picks`/`activity` sections, and the reviewer-in-runtime decision below.
4. **Reviewer in the runtime (Jev) v1, or a decision not to build it.** Say whether a reviewer runs in the hosted path, where (after the model assessment, before the policy), what it may do (veto only, never raise a forecast), its failure policy (the `block-case` question left open in `BTC-DECISION-BENCHMARK-VALIDATION.md:158-163`), its cost accounting, its acceptance gate before it is enabled (heldout, real captured cases, human labels), and how it appears in the trace. Depends on the benchmark reaching heldout, the observation captures, and the monetary budget (spec 5).
5. **Research spend budget v1.** Reserve micro-USD against the agent's ledger before each model and search call, reconcile it to reported usage, and fail closed when usage is unknown. Say what the owner sees and how it relates to `spend.perCallAusd/perDayAusd`, which caps paper trades, not research. Depends on `PostgresBudgetLedger`, provider tariffs per model, and the runner's `prepareStep` guard.
6. **Market scope expansion v1.** Define the next category after sports (crypto up/down, from the BTC laboratories; general Polymarket categories; Perpl perps), its protocol, prompts, plugins and acceptance, and the "Soon" to "live" rule. Depends on the trading architecture and the Perpl data plugin (open PRs #60, #63).
7. **Agent API (HMAC) purpose v1.** Decide whether agents outside the in-process runtime (external runtimes, SDK clients) are a product. If they are, specify secret issuance and rotation, kill/pause, event ingestion, budget reservation over HMAC, and contract tests against the Relayer drift items D1–D8. If not, remove the dormant routes. Depends on the answer to open question 5.
8. **Exit monitoring v1.** Evaluate `exitsWhen` after entry, on a schedule or on price moves, and close paper positions, later live ones. Depends on paper positions, scheduling, and spec 1.
9. **Scheduling v2 (follow-ups, calendar).** The unbuilt part of agent-runtime-v1 "Scheduling": bounded agent-requested follow-ups, timezone and DST rules, event-triggered runs. Depends on the funding lock and spec 5.
10. **Remote research acceptance record.** Not a design spec but a missing artifact: one manual, one manual and one scheduled paid run on staging, recorded with Worker versions (`docs/cloudflare-staging.md:168-174`).

## 5. Open questions for the owner

1. Is "reviewing decisions" a feature for the **creator** in the Studio, for **buyers** on the marketplace, for an internal **operator/QA** role, or for all three? The answer decides specs 2 and 3.
2. Should decisions become public at all, and when: at once, after the market closes, or never for agents that sell reads?
3. Does a Jev (or other model) reviewer belong in the product runtime? If so, veto only? Given the stopped benchmark, what acceptance gate must it pass first?
4. Should the BTC benchmark continue with the prepared `invalidAnswerPolicy: "block-case"`, which needs explicit approval and budget, or is the laboratory track paused?
5. Is the agent HMAC API (from Relayer) still part of Pickler, or does the in-process runtime replace it?
6. After sports, which market comes next? Is Perpl perps the target for the brief's `maxLeverage`?
7. Should research spend (model and Exa) be charged to the agent's funded balance, to the platform, or to the creator?
8. Should abstentions and policy-held trades count in an agent's public record? The laboratories score them (Brier includes abstainers).
9. Should the pending staging acceptance runs be authorized now, before more runtime features ship?
